04 · Why it costs what it costs
Pricing logic

You're paying for
what it brings back.

Not hours. A cut of the revenue lift the site drives over three years. That's why the price lives on a fit call — I need your numbers first.

The rule of thumb
Roughly 10% of the annual revenue lift the site is expected to drive.

Two extra customers a month at your margin — that's the number the site is worth. Price is a slice of that, paid once. Not rent forever.

Why no list price
A $400/yr side hustle and a $500K shop shouldn't pay the same.

Same build, different backing. Quoting blind is how agencies overcharge small ops and underdeliver to bigger ones.
