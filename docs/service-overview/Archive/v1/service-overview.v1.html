Where these numbers come from

Sources & Methodology.

Every range in this overview comes from published 2026 Canadian pricing data, with one US source for cross-reference. All figures are CAD unless marked. Click through and verify.

01
Canada Web Pro — Small Business Website Costs in Canada (2026) · CAD
canadawebpro.ca/small-business-website-cost-in-canada-2026
Used for: Canadian SMB website ranges — basic from $1,500, standard $3,500–$5,000, advanced $6,000–$10,000+. Backs Launch and Foundation comparison strips.
02
Impeka — Web Design Pricing in Canada (2026) · CAD
impeka.com/web-design-pricing-canada
Used for: Canadian agency tier pricing — small-business sites $3,000–$8,000, mid-sized corporate $8,000–$25,000. Cross-references the Foundation and Authority advanced tiers.
03
DesignEdge — Website Maintenance Plan Cost in 2026 · CAD
designedge.ca/website-maintenance-plan-cost-in-2026-a-strategic-budgeting-guide
Used for: Canadian monthly retainer tiers — basic $100–$300/mo, growth $300–$800/mo, e-commerce $1,000+/mo. Ad-hoc support $90–$175/hr. Compared against FJMedia Maintain $150 / Grow $300.
04
Clickworthy — Local SEO Cost in Canada (2026) · CAD
clickworthy.io/blog/how-much-does-local-seo-cost-for-canadian-small-businesses-in-2026
Used for: Canadian local SEO monthly tiers — basic $340–$690/mo, mid $690–$1,380/mo, comprehensive $1,380–$2,750+/mo. Foundation covers on-page SEO; Authority adds ongoing monitoring.
05
Clutch — Web Design Company Pricing Guide (2026) · USD, reference
clutch.co/web-designers/pricing
Used for: Industry-wide cross-reference. Most web design projects on Clutch cost less than $10,000 USD; platform average is $38,105 USD. USD converts ~35% higher in CAD, which makes Canadian comparisons more favourable.
Methodology. FJMedia prices sit below Canadian mid-range while the Winnipeg case study portfolio builds out. At 8–10 verified cases, package pricing moves to market rate. Retainers already sit at the Canadian low end. Every CAD range on the comparison strips matches a published tier from the sources cited above — no figures are extrapolated or rounded beyond what the sources state. If a source contradicts a claim, the claim gets updated.
