# Ferrari Framework — FJMedia Agency Site Redesign

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Status:** Approved (pending implementation plan)

---

## Overview

FJMedia is switching from the "We build your website for free" offer to the **Ferrari Framework** — a two-lane sales model that separates the design (shell) from the backend (engine). The agency site gets a full copy and structure overhaul to reflect this.

### The Ferrari Framework (Internal SOP — Not Client-Facing)

- **The Shell** = the visual design, hand-coded, delivered in 48 hours after a discovery call
- **The Engine** = backend systems (forms, CRM, email automation, SEO) sold as packages after shell approval
- **Two lanes:**
  - **Simple clients** (events, landing pages, linktrees) → buy the shell, done
  - **Business clients** → buy the shell, then pick a package to get the engine
- Shell price ($200-300) absorbs into higher package tiers — no double-charging

### Core Messaging Shift

- **Old:** "We build your website for free. Built in 5 days. Pay only if you like it."
- **New:** "We'll cook you up a website in 48 hours. Free to see it. Pay if you're satisfied."
- **Key differentiator to hammer:** Hand-coded, no Wix, no Shopify, no templates

### SEO Strategy

- Target keywords: "web design Winnipeg", "hand-coded web design", "custom website Winnipeg", "Wix alternative", "custom website not Shopify"
- H1 carries personality, SEO line below CTA carries keywords
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness + ProfessionalService targeting Winnipeg

### Voice Rules

- Headlines: punchy, personality, James's voice
- Body copy: clear and direct, not corporate but not slang
- CTAs: first-person when possible ("Get My Free Design")
- Never say: "solutions," "leverage," "synergy," "optimize your digital presence"
- Always say: "website" not "web solution," "hand-coded" not "custom-developed"

## Section 4: Process

**01 — Quick Call**
*15 minutes. That's it.*
A quick call to learn about your business and what you need. No pitch, no commitment — just seeing if we're the right fit.

**02 — We Design It**
*48 hours. No payment. No contract.*
We get to work and hand-code your website from scratch. 48 hours later, you're looking at a live preview — before you've spent a cent.

**03 — You Decide**
*Love it or leave it.*
We send you the link. Share it with whoever you want. If you're satisfied, we move forward. If not — walk away, no charge.

**04 — Go Live**
*Your site. Your rules.*
Happy with the design? You're live. Need more — ordering forms, lead capture, SEO, the full backend? We walk you through packages built on top of what we already made together. Everything adds on. Nothing starts over.
