Policies

Payment

We accept Cash, Debit, Visa and Mastercard.

Guest

Our doors remain locked until the time of your appointment. Please send us a text if you arrive early so we can unlock the door for you.

Late Policy

Late appointment arrival may result in a shortened treatment. We understand there may be times of unforeseen circumstances. We kindly ask that you contact us to make necessary adjustments to your appointment.

Cancellation

Treatment times are reserved especially for you. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment will result in a cancellation fee equal to 100% of the reserved services. The cancellation fee also applies to no-shows. If illness or unforeseen circumstances occur, please contact us at jtranbeauty@gmail.com as soon as possible — we'll be happy to reschedule.

Before Your

Appointment

Arrive Fresh-Faced

Please come with clean skin, free of makeup, heavy creams, or SPF for the best results.

Skip Active Products

Avoid using retinol, exfoliants, or acids at least 48 hours before your treatment.

Stay Sun Safe

Limit direct sun exposure before and after your appointment to protect your skin.

Share Your Skin Story

Please inform me of any allergies, medications, or recent treatments so I can customize your skin care.
