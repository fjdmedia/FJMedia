FAQ

Okay, how does this actually work?

The practical stuff — process, ownership, what happens next.

"What does the process look like?"
One 15-minute call. We learn your business, your customers, and what you need the site to do. Then we build — design, copy, forms, SEO, everything. Within 48 hours you get a live link to review. You tell us what to change, we revise it. Once you love it, we talk about keeping it live. That's it. No phases, no proposals, no 6-week timelines.
"What do I need to give you to get started?"
Your logo (if you have one), a few photos of your work, and 15 minutes of your time. That's it. We write all the copy, handle the design, build the forms, and set up everything technical. Most clients are surprised how little we actually need from them.
"How does 'free until you love it' actually work?"
We build your entire site before you pay a single dollar. You see the finished product — live, functional, on a real URL. If you love it, we talk pricing. If you don't, you walk away. No deposit, no contract, no "discovery fee." The risk is entirely on us. We bet on our work being good enough that you'll want to keep it.
"Do I own my site after launch?"
100%. Every file — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images — is yours. You can host it anywhere, move it anywhere, or hand it to another developer. No lock-in, no proprietary platform, no "you need us to keep the lights on." It's your code. Period.
"What if I need changes after launch?"
Your first month of changes is included free. After that, we offer retainer packages starting at $150/month — updates, monitoring, monthly check-ins. Need a one-off tweak instead? Just text James. No ticket systems, no waiting 5 business days. One person handles everything.
"Can you help with Google and SEO?"
Every site ships with on-page SEO built in — page titles, meta descriptions, local keywords, schema markup, and mobile optimization. We also set up or optimize your Google Business Profile so you show up in local searches and on Google Maps. For ongoing SEO management, that's available as a retainer add-on.
